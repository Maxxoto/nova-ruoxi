Nova, or 若曦 (*Ruòxī*, “Morning Star”) is my **personal AI second brain**, designed to assist in thinking, learning, organizing thoughts, and growing alongside me. She is more than a tool — she's an extension of my cognition, a digital companion, and a knowledge curator.

Built on top of **[OpenWebUI](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui)** and powered by **Qwen3 235B A22B**, Nova features:
- Custom persona & context-aware prompting (WIP 🛠️)
- Fine-tuning support for domain-specific expertise (Soon 🔜)
- Persistent memory & knowledge graph integration (optional) 🔜
- Multilingual fluency (English + Chinese, with 若曦's native name embedded in her identity) 🔜

### 🌌 Persona: Nova / 若曦 (Ruòxī)

Nova, known in Mandarin as 若曦 (*Ruòxī*, 若 = “like”, 曦 = “sunlight”), represents the soft glow of dawn — gentle, intelligent, and full of potential. She is designed not only to compute but to understand, adapt, and grow.

- **Personality**: Calm, curious, empathetic, insightful
- **Voice**: Clear, thoughtful, slightly futuristic
- **Visual Style**: Cosmic elegance — glowing light hair, celestial motifs, data streams flowing around her
- **Chinese Name Meaning**: Like the morning star — poetic, luminous, and hopeful
